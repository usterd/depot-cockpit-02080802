Which statuses to import?

Rows that are not selected are never stored in the first place. At Scalable, cancelled and pending orders carry no amounts – they only document the attempt. Rows already imported are kept; use “Remove cancelled” below to clean up.

◆ Depot Cockpit

What it is for

A private cockpit for your securities portfolio: positions, realized gains by FIFO, distributions, fees and taxes – as an overview, a table and a calendar. No prices are fetched from the internet; everything is calculated purely from what you import.

What you need

The transaction export from Scalable Capital as a CSV file.
In the broker under Account → Transactions → Export, pick a period and save the file.

Multiple files and partial periods are no problem: the app detects duplicate rows itself and skips them. Import under Import / Data.

As an app on your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari (Chrome cannot do it).
  2. Let it load completely once, while you have internet.
  3. Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
  4. From then on it starts like an app – even in airplane mode.

Your data stays here

The app sends no financial data. Everything lives in this browser's storage, on this device. There is no server, no account, no background analysis.

Nothing leaves unless you trigger it yourself – a CSV export or a backup via the share menu.

Important: browser storage is not permanent. Save a backup regularly under Import / Data → Save backup (JSON).
